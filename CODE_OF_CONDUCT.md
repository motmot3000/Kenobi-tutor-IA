# Code of conduct

Kenobi is a tool for people who are learning. Its community should feel the same way a good classroom does: safe to
ask, safe to be wrong.

## Our pledge

We, contributors and maintainers, pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- explaining patiently, especially to beginners;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback on the work, never on the person;
- assuming good faith and asking before concluding;
- owning our mistakes and learning from them.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal or political
attacks, publishing others' private information without their explicit permission, and any conduct that would be
inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow
this code of conduct, and may ban contributors temporarily or permanently for repeated or serious breaches.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately through the repository's **Security** tab (› Report a vulnerability, with
"Code of conduct" in the title) or to a maintainer directly. All reports are reviewed promptly and confidentially.
